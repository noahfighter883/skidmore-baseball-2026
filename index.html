What the stats mean

OPS
On-base percentage plus slugging: how often a hitter gets on and how much damage he does.
ISO
Isolated power, slugging minus average. Extra bases per at-bat.
WHIP
Walks plus hits allowed per inning.
K/9 and BB/9
Strikeouts and walks per nine innings.
IP
Innings pitched. 43.2 means 43 and 2/3 innings.
One-run game
Any game decided by exactly one run. "Best ERA among starters" counts pitchers with 20+ innings.

All numbers are the official 2026 cumulative stats from skidmoreathletics.com. Liberty League games are the 15 against Bard, Rensselaer, Ithaca, Vassar and Union. Download games.csv
