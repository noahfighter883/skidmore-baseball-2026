What the stats mean

OPS is on-base percentage plus slugging percentage, one number for how often a hitter gets on and how much damage he does. WHIP is walks plus hits allowed per inning. K/9 and BB/9 are strikeouts and walks per nine innings. A one-run game is any game decided by exactly one run. "Best ERA among starters" counts pitchers with 20+ innings.

All numbers are the official 2026 cumulative stats from skidmoreathletics.com. Liberty League games are the 15 against Bard, Rensselaer, Ithaca, Vassar and Union.
